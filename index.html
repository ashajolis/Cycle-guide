Days 1–4 · Inner Winter

Menstrual Phase

Your period days. Hormones at their lowest. Your rhythm stays the same but the intensity of every block drops. Rest is the work right now.

"I release. I rest. I renew.
Stillness is not laziness — it is wisdom."
Block-by-Block Adjustments
⏰

How Each Block Shifts

  • Wizard Hour: No movement. Warm lemon water, heating pad, gentle journaling. Write what you want to release this cycle.
  • Self Care: Extra slow. Comfortable clothes always. No pressure to look "put together."
  • Breakfast: Iron-rich foods every day — eggs, spinach, lentils, red meat. Ginger or raspberry leaf tea for cramps.
  • Quick Tidy Up: Dishes only. Ask partner to handle the rest Day 1–2. Done is enough.
  • Homeschool: Full cozy mode. Audiobooks, documentaries, read-alouds on the sofa. Apps for independent learning.
  • Home Making + Film: Day 1–2: chores only — no filming. Day 3–4: quiet vlog or voiceover only if you feel okay.
  • Rest: Full 60 min every day. Heat pad. This is medicine — not optional.
  • Girls Time: Board games, puzzles, books, movies. Low energy, high warmth.
  • House Reset: Partner and kids own this block Day 1–2. You supervise from the sofa.
  • Dish Washing: Partner does this all 4 days. Standing arrangement.
  • Bed Time: Start wind-down at 8:30pm. Epsom bath + magnesium + chamomile tea.

Days 23–24 · Your Unique Gift

Your Nesting Days

The 2 days before your period when you get a burst of energy specifically for cleaning and organizing everything. This is real, hormonal, and yours to use intentionally.

Why It Happens

🧬 The Science Behind Your Surge

On Days 23–24, progesterone and estrogen crash together. This triggers a spike in cortisol and adrenaline — your body's "get ready" hormones. Instead of pure anxiety, your system channels this as physical, environment-controlling energy. Your brain also craves dopamine to offset the serotonin crash of late luteal — and cleaning and organizing deliver exactly that reward. Evolutionarily it mirrors pre-birth nesting in animals. Your period is your monthly reset, and your body prepares the space for it. This is not random. This is you.

Your Nesting Day Rhythm (Days 23–24)
5:00 am — Wizard Hour
Gentle yoga + journaling. Write your nesting list. Prioritize 3 big tasks. Don't begin yet — let the surge build.
7:00–9:00 am — Breakfast + Tidy Up
Normal blocks but you'll notice the urge already. Let the tidy-up go slightly longer today — wipe counters, clear surfaces. The kids can help sort things into piles.
10:00 am — Homeschool (Light)
Set up independent work — apps, audiobook, art project. You're in and out. The girls know today is a "nesting day" — they can participate in organizing their own room.
~11:00 am — Nesting Block 1 (90 min)
Your biggest cleaning task of the day. Play a podcast or music. Deep clean kitchen, bathrooms, or whatever calls loudest. Kids can help wipe, sort, and carry — make it a team game.
12:00 pm — Meals + Rest (Full Stop)
Lunch, then your full rest hour. You've done the hard part. The surge will try to keep you going — honour your body and stop.
2:00 pm — Nesting Block 2 (60 min)
Organizing, decluttering, period kit prep, meal prep. Smaller and more detailed tasks. Prep your comfort basket for your period days.
4:00 pm — Rest (Non-negotiable)
The nesting surge fades fast. Honour the stop. Rest now so your period days are easier.
Nesting Master Checklist
🧹

Save These Tasks for Days 23–24

  • Kitchen deep clean — inside fridge, wipe cabinets, organize pantry
  • Bathrooms — grout, behind toilet, restock all supplies
  • Girls' rooms reset — declutter toys, organize shelves, rotate items
  • Laundry blitz — wash, dry, fold AND put away completely
  • Batch cook — soups, grains, snacks for your period days
  • Period kit prep — restock period products, heat packs, magnesium, dark chocolate, cozy socks, favorite tea
  • Homeschool prep — set out next week's materials, sharpen pencils, organize books
  • UGC workspace — clean filming space, prep props, organize content folders
  • Home admin — pay bills, file papers, clear email inbox
  • Car clean-out or one declutter/donate bag
🌙 The Gift of NestingWhen you work with these 2 days, your period arrives to a clean home, prepped meals, restocked supplies, and organized space. You have taken care of your future self. That is love in action.

Content Creator System

UGC by Phase

Your content business fully synced to your cycle. Film when you're magnetic. Edit when you're detail-oriented. Rest when you need it. Stop fighting your biology.

🩸 Menstrual (Days 1–4) — Admin Only

  • Respond to brand emails at your own pace
  • Review contracts and briefs — your intuition is sharpest now
  • Write future content ideas in your journal
  • Audit last cycle's analytics — what performed, what didn't
  • No filming. No pitching. No camera. No pressure.

🌱 Follicular (Days 5–11) — Create & Pitch

  • Write all your scripts for the cycle — brain creates best now
  • Batch film 4–6 pieces across 2–3 sessions in your 2pm Home Making block
  • Pitch 3–5 new brands — your writing is confident and fresh
  • Try a new content format you've been curious about
  • Organize your content calendar for the full 25 days ahead
  • Plan your filming day outfits and props for ovulation week

✨ Ovulatory (Days 12–16) — Film Everything

  • All camera-facing content goes here. Talking heads, reviews, demos, unboxings, brand content.
  • Go live — your energy is electric in real time
  • Negotiate rates with brands — you're most confident and articulate
  • Reach out to dream brands — the fear genuinely feels smaller now
  • Film while doing your 2pm Home Making block — clip mic + natural content
  • Take brand photos — skin, energy, and confidence at their absolute peak

🍂 Early Luteal (Days 17–21) — Edit & Deliver

  • Edit everything filmed in ovulation — your detail eye is sharpest here
  • Write captions, add hashtags, finalize deliverables
  • Submit content to brands, send invoices
  • Voiceovers, sit-down reviews, behind-the-scenes — works well
  • Schedule posts for the coming weeks using your 2pm block
  • Respond to comments and engage with your audience

🪺 Late Luteal / Nesting (Days 22–25) — Minimum

  • Scheduled posts only — set and forget
  • No new pitches, no new commitments, no new filming
  • On nesting Days 23–24: organize your filming space and props while you have the energy
  • Write down content ideas as they come — your subconscious is very active
  • Protect your energy. Your period is coming. You've already done the work.
💡 The Cycle Creator Method Plan in menstrual → Create & pitch in follicular → Film everything in ovulatory → Edit & deliver in luteal → Rest in late luteal. When you stop fighting your cycle and flow with it, your output increases while your effort decreases.
